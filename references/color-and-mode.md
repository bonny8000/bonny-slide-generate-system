# Color And Mode

Use color semantically. A slide should usually have one accent family plus neutrals.

## Base Neutrals

- Light canvas: `#FFFFFF`, `#F5F7FB`, `#F1F4F8`.
- Light card: `#FFFFFF`.
- Light text: `#111827`, `#2D3748`, `#667085`.
- Dark canvas: `#111418`, `#171B22`, `#202632`, `#2F3A48`.
- Dark card: `#1D232B`, `#262D36`, `#343C47`.
- Dark text: `#FFFFFF`, `#E6EAF0`, `#A8B0BD`.

## Accent Families

- Blue `#0D7FF2`, `#2F80ED`, `#4C83F1`: research, data, AI, system clarity, mobility, product structure.
- Green `#20C781`, `#31D48A`, `#52DFA7`: improvement, success, service simplification, welfare, growth, sustainability.
- Purple `#6B6FF2`, `#7A72F2`: social, motivation, friendly AI, portfolio softness.
- Orange/yellow `#FF9F1C`, `#FFD84D`: energy, study, play, attention, warm service tone.
- Pink/red `#FF5C8A`, `#EF476F`, `#E74C3C`: pain, warning, performance issue, frustration, negative test result.
- Gray `#D8DEE8`, `#98A2B3`: inactive states, baselines, unselected categories.

## Light Mode Rules

Use light mode for:

- Background, desk research, survey evidence, normal user research, data cards, MVP feature explanation, and service keyword pages.

Treatment:

- Keep most of the slide white or pale gray.
- Use white cards with soft shadow or very light borders.
- Use accent color for headings, chart highlights, tabs, bottom insight bands, and key metrics.
- Use shadows sparingly. Prefer `0 16px 40px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- Use pale tinted panels behind grouped content.

## Dark Mode Rules

Use dark mode for:

- Strong insights, user-test findings, result dashboards, emotional interview boards, workflow/status maps, mobile app walkthroughs, product reveal sections, and solution climax slides.

Treatment:

- Use deep neutral backgrounds, not pure black.
- Use white headlines and muted gray body text.
- Use one bright accent for the core meaning.
- Use translucent panels only when the background image is dimmed enough for text contrast.
- Use bright green/blue metrics against dark backgrounds for result slides.
- Use white cards on dark backgrounds when showing phone UI, forms, quotes, or feature modules.

## Gradient Rules

Gradients are allowed only when they support atmosphere or grouping.

- Use soft pastel gradients for pain point/opportunity slides, not dense research data.
- Use dark image overlays for cinematic insight or product walkthrough slides.
- Do not use loud rainbow gradients or decorative blobs.
- Do not let gradients reduce chart readability.

## Accent Selection By Slide Intent

- Research/data: blue.
- Problem/pain: pink/red, or gray with one red marker.
- Solution/improvement: green or blue.
- Product features: blue, green, or neutral navy depending on brand.
- Motivation/social/play: purple, orange, or yellow.
- Results: green for positive improvement, blue for system/product lift, red/pink for reduced risk or friction.

## Contrast Checks

- Body text on light cards should be at least `#475467` or darker.
- Body text on dark slides should be at least `#D0D5DD`.
- Avoid low-opacity text under 60% unless it is decorative metadata.
- In data charts, inactive bars can be gray, but active bars need enough contrast to be read from a presentation screen.
