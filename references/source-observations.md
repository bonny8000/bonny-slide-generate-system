## Uploaded Reference Themes

### Korean UX Portfolio Light Mode

- Pale gray or white background.
- Bold Korean headline, small category labels, and muted supporting text.
- Rounded white cards with soft shadows.
- Blue is the dominant research/data accent.
- Evidence is often grouped as 2-3 cards with charts, survey snippets, or quote cards.

### Dark Mode Product / UX Case Study

- Deep charcoal or blue-gray background.
- White headline, gray body, bright green/blue accent.
- Common uses: interview synthesis, user test result, solution reveal, app walkthrough, result data.
- Phone mockups and white UI cards sit on dark canvas with dashed callout lines.

### Research To Insight Flow

- Background and desk research slides are light, structured, and evidence-heavy.
- Insight slides often switch to dark mode or a stronger band to mark synthesis.
- Solution slides map pain to opportunity or As-Is to To-Be.

### Mobile Product Explanation

- Use phone mockups as primary visuals.
- Show one screen state per feature.
- Annotate with callout dots, side labels, and small captions.
- Do not overload the slide with app screens.

### Result / Achieved Slides

- Results use big percentages, time reductions, and comparison bars.
- Dark result dashboards feel more executive and dramatic.
- Light achieved cards feel more client-report oriented.

## Repeated Components

- Three evidence cards.
- Quote bubbles and speech cards.
- Insight trio.
- Key insight bottom band.
- As-Is / To-Be comparison.
- Phone mockup plus annotations.
- Horizontal timeline with stage cards.
- Positioning map with feature list.
- Large metric cards.
- Split light/dark before/after pages.

## Style Boundaries

- Do not copy the Korean language content directly. Re-express in Traditional Chinese and English.
- Do not overuse cute emoji/character elements; use them only for approachable portfolio or user-empathy slides.
- Do not turn every slide into a card grid. Use cards when evidence needs grouping.
- Preserve Bonny's core rule: clarity first, decoration second.
