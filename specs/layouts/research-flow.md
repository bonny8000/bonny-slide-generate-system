# research-flow

## Purpose
Tell a research story top-to-bottom: background data → a reframed question (HMW) → desk-research evidence.

## When to use / When NOT
Use to connect data to a "how might we" and then to supporting literature. **Not** for a single stat,
and **not** for survey result sets (use survey-stack).

## Structure
Three stacked sections, separated by a small downward connector:
1. **Background** — eyebrow + headline; a row of 2–3 **data cards** (bar / bubble-cluster / donut-with-number).
2. **HMW** — a full-width **callout-band** holding the reframed question; one keyword in `--accent`.
3. **Desk Research** — headline + 2–3 **citation-cards** (finding + source).

## Tokens used
canvas, surface (data/citation cards), ink (headlines/findings), muted (captions/sources),
accent (one figure/keyword), accent-soft or band-fill (the HMW band).

## Icon use
None — the data, the band, and the citations carry it.

## Content rules
One idea per section. HMW is phrased as a question. Citations = finding sentence + source line (muted).
繁中 primary; figures Latin.

## Do / Don't
Do keep the three sections visually distinct and connected. Don't stack more than 3 cards per section;
don't let the HMW band hold more than the question.
